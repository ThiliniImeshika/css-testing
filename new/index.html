Cherry blossoms, or sakura

Cherry blossoms, or sakura, are delicate flowers that symbolize the fleeting nature of life. They are celebrated for their breathtaking beauty and are a significant part of Japanese culture, particularly during the spring festival known as Hanami. Cherry blossoms bloom for only a short period, typically lasting from late March to early May, and are often associated with the concept of mono no aware, which refers to the awareness of impermanence. This tradition encourages people to appreciate the beauty of life and the present moment, making cherry blossoms a timeless symbol of hope and renewal.
